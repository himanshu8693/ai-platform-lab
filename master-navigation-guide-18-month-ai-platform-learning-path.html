Every topic, every week, in exact execution order — DevOps Projects P1–25 · AI-Roadmap Phases 1–6 · MCP servers · IBM Context Forge · Zero-Trust Hardening Sprint (ADR-008–016) · Skills Coverage G1–G7 · 3 certs · 3 capstones · 3 blog posts · job applications

What changed in this revision A simulated Staff-level architecture panel reviewed the Zero-Trust AI Execution Flow (ADR-005/006/007) and identified 8 concrete gaps: semantic authorization (Gap A), workload identity / confused deputy (Gap B), unmeasured latency (Gap C), no output-side DLP (Gap D), no audit correlation (Gap E), no offline/air-gapped delivery story (Gap F), no incumbent-PAM federation story (Gap G), and no CAB-compatible GitOps story (Gap H). IBM Bob generated ADR-008 through ADR-015, two FDE client-simulation scenarios, one compliance runbook, and a latency benchmark harness to close them — all of which already exist in the repo but were not yet sequenced into this guide. This revision inserts a 6-week Zero-Trust Hardening Sprint immediately after the Context Forge milestone (end of Month 12) and before GPU Fleet Ops (Month 13). Total plan length moves from 18 to ~19.5 months. Every "Month N" label from the original Month 13 onward now happens roughly 1.5 months later than the label states — treat the numbers below as sequence position, not literal calendar months, past that point.
How to use this document
  1. Start here, always. This is the single source of truth for sequencing. All other appendices (A–I) are referenced from within it — you never need to open them speculatively.
  2. Two parallel tracks, never swapped. Weekday evenings → AI-Roadmap. Saturdays → DevOps Projects. Sundays → writing (ADRs, blog posts, READMEs).
  3. Every row has a gate. Do not advance to the next step until the gate for the current step passes. A gate is always a running system or a committed file — never just "I understand it."
  4. MCP (Months 11–12) and Context Forge (Month 12) are placed exactly where they appear in this guide. They are not optional extras — they are the capstone that converts the portfolio from "DevOps with AI sprinkled in" to "AI Platform Engineer who built a production AI gateway."
  5. The Zero-Trust Hardening Sprint (Hardening Wk H1–H6) is not optional either. It sits immediately after Context Forge and before GPU Fleet Ops. Skipping it leaves the portfolio at "built a working MCP demo" — the hardening sprint is what proves the demo survives adversarial review, which is the exact bar a Staff-level panel holds you to.
  6. Open the full timeline HTML (unified-learning-path-devops-ai-platform-18-month-realistic-timeline.html) for the step-by-step detail of each block. This guide gives you the map; that document gives you the turn-by-turn directions.
